# Warranty Policy

All Nexa Electronics phones, laptops, headphones, and smartwatches include a 12-month limited warranty from the delivery date. Selected accessories include a 6-month warranty, as shown on the product page or invoice.

The warranty covers manufacturing defects under normal use. It does not cover accidental damage, liquid damage, unauthorized repairs, cosmetic wear, or problems caused by misuse. Customers should contact support with their order number and a description of the issue. Nexa may repair or replace an eligible item after inspection.
