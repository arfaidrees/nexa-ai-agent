# Shipping and Delivery

Standard delivery is free for orders of PKR 10,000 or more. Orders below PKR 10,000 have a PKR 250 delivery fee.

Delivery usually takes 2 to 5 business days in major cities and 4 to 8 business days for other areas in Pakistan. Business days exclude public holidays. Customers receive tracking details by email or SMS after dispatch.

If an order arrives damaged, contact Nexa support within 48 hours with photos of the parcel and product so the team can investigate promptly.
